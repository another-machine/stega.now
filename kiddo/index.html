kiddo

five home video moments representing one decade of my childhood, encoded into five images.

each image carries the audio, video frames, and a note to my past self. there are no video or audio files, just the image. tap an image and watch it come back as it plays.

tools provided by another machine.

The visual distortion in these images is the result of an audio waveform, image frames (6fps), and text, stored in image pixels. They were created using code libraries that can be found at amplib.app. The same tools are used here to decode and play them back as sound and video.

I am jake, and I started another machine.
