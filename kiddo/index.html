kiddo

five minutes of home video, held in five pictures.

each picture carries its own clip: the sound, the frames, and a note i wrote back to the kid in them. nothing here is a video file. select a year and watch the picture come back as it plays.

tools provided by another machine.

the pictures are the only files here. the sound, the frames of the clip, and the text all live in their pixels, and the visible distortion is that payload. reading it back develops the picture again, in the order it was written.

the clips run at six frames a second, 128 pixels wide. that is what fits — a picture holds what a picture holds.

my name is jake, and i started another machine to create interesting things.
