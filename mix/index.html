stegassette/mix

loops that know what they are. a mix stegassette is one seamlessly looping bar of music carrying its own tempo, key, and the position of its first beat — so any number of them, made by anyone, drop together and play in time and in key. the audio is the first entry, so every one of them is still an ordinary stegassette: drop it in the plain player and it plays while the picture develops.

the mix

grid is the global cycle in beats — every loop starts and relaunches on a multiple of it. 1 is immediate, 16 is phrase-aligned. Bars are not involved; a loop can be any number of beats long.
scale matches the seven notes, so F minor sits in an A♭ major mix untouched. tonic matches the declared roots instead, which is what a bassline wants.
