grid is the global cycle in beats — every loop starts and relaunches on a multiple of it. 1 is immediate, 16 is phrase-aligned. Bars are not involved; a loop can be any number of beats long.
scale matches the seven notes, so F minor sits in an A♭ major mix untouched. tonic matches the declared roots instead, which is what a bassline wants.
